Stock-Based Compensation
For the three months ended June 30, 2025 and 2026, total stock-based compensation (SBC) expense was $6.0 billion and $8.0 billion, including amounts associated with awards we expect to settle in Alphabet stock of $5.8 billion and $7.6 billion, respectively. For the six months ended June 30, 2025 and 2026, total SBC expense was $11.5 billion and $15.2 billion, including amounts associated with awards we expect to settle in Alphabet stock of $11.1 billion and $14.1 billion, respectively.
